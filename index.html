The team

Built by an engineer. Driven by everyone who joins in.

545 Motorsport was founded by Susanna, an engineer with a lifelong love of racing and the patience to do it properly.

The plan is simple to say and hard to do: climb the ladder from club racing to international GT endurance racing, line up on the grid at La Sarthe, and take the chequered flag at the 24 Hours of Le Mans by 2035.

Every part of the team runs the way an engineer would run it. Measure, learn, improve, repeat. No shortcuts, no noise, just steady progress towards the biggest race in the world.
