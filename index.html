The team

Built to win the biggest race in the world

545 Motorsport was founded by Susanna, a trained engineer with a lifelong love of racing and the patience to do it properly.

The plan is clear: climb from club racing to international GT endurance racing, take our place on the grid at La Sarthe, and win the 24 Hours of Le Mans by 2035.

Every decision is measured, tested and improved. No shortcuts and no noise, just steady progress towards one chequered flag.
